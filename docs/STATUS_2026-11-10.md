## B2. P2P — notify and gate, THROUGH THE MARKETPLACE, with DOWNLOAD (operator rulings 2026-11-10) — EDITED, PARSE-ONLY, not deployed
**Terminology:** "paired" in the P2P module = the operator-approved peer allow-list (`paired_peers.json`, `/network/approve-peer`). It is NOT device pairing (paired devices sync natively, separately). The MARKETPLACE flows among **connected peers** (no approval needed — it is a catalog); every other P2P message kind still requires an approved peer (default-deny unchanged).
Announcements are NOT sent to each peer one by one: an item is published to THIS instance's marketplace listing and ONE push goes to every connected peer.
