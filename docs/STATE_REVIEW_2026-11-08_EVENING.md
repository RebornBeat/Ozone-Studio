**I2 — Where the quota actually goes: our own votes, not walks.** Of today's 334 logged successful calls, **`strength_n_vote` = 217 (65 %)**, `keyword_settle` 44, `methodology_synthesis` 25, `walk:pipeline-9` **12**, AMT 16. The E13 relevance filter runs ≥ 1 vote per gathered methodology (a kept one costs 1 call, a dropped one 5) **sequentially**, which made **Stage 3 take 448–523 s** (27 methodologies) in the span-walks — a latency regression I introduced. The right shape is the one Pass C already uses: **propose in ONE batched call, confirm only the proposed drops with the strength-N vote** (1 + 5 × proposed instead of 27 × (1..5)); the same applies to the E14 rule gate and the capability-necessity votes.
**I3 — The system knows the quota is exhausted and keeps spending effort on it.** `openrouter_quota` logs EXHAUSTED, yet AMT re-expansion, the I-loop, settlement and the meta review keep issuing calls that fail with 429 (401 `free-models-per-day` lines; `AMT re-expansion attempt failed — will retry`, `I-Loop reflection pass failed`). Background actors should check `openrouter_quota::latest().remaining` before model work and sleep to the UTC reset.
**I4 — Reframing the "key rotation / paid tier" lever.** The account is already funded ($48.58) and on the 1,000/day free-model tier; the exhaustion is mostly self-inflicted background consumption (I2). Levers, in order of value: (1) batching + quota-gated background actors (engineering, no policy needed); (2) an operator-approved, cost-capped **paid fallback** when the free quota is exhausted (today paid overrides are refused by doctrine); (3) rotation is not the lever.
**I5 — 507 of 523 searches carry no trace and no origin.** Only walk-originated searches (16, `orchestrate`) have either; the rest come from background actors (AMT loop, ripple/assistant, test traffic) that were never wrapped with a trace/origin. Consequences: (a) they cannot join to outcomes, so the dataset has **6 groups**; (b) they inflate `returned`, so **hotness = packed ÷ returned is diluted** by background searches that can never be `packed`. Fix: wrap the AMT loop / assistant / ripple actors with `Origin::actor` (+ a trace), and compute hotness against TRACED `returned` only (a `returned_traced` counter), keeping `access_frequency` as total.
**I6 — Query-side noise in the training data.** The one real group's `query_terms` contain filler (`the`, `you`, `what`, `each`, `does`, `one`), `you (implied)` and a **13-word whole sentence** — span-out builds its keywords from `step.context_requirements` "as given" (AMT branch keywords), bypassing the shape rule. Apply `is_sentence_like` / marker / token expansion at span-out (and ideally where AMT generates those keywords).
**I7 — Pass C (zero-shot, stoplist-free) works exactly as the operator wanted.** All 14 unanimous removals are English filler tokens (`the`, `a`, `for`, `from`, `be`, `is`, `that`) sitting in methodology keywords; borderline generics (`best`, `practices`, `must`, `read`, `full`, `content`, `standard`) were kept because the votes were not unanimous. **Root cause is at creation:** methodology keywords are derived by tokenising the TITLE ("Trace the Full Write-to-Read Path"). Fix at the source (the creating pipeline/meta drafter should not emit title fragments), so Pass C is a cleanup, not the defence.
**I8 — Expansion is add-only and can over-add.** 1,117 expansion terms went onto ~114 methodologies (≈ 10 each); one container received 20 near-duplicate phrases all prefixed with its own subject (`methodology governance`, `methodology audit`, …). Needs a near-duplicate / token-subset dedupe and a measured effect on precision before it is trusted at scale.
**I9 — Promotion is mathematically blocked, not just data-starved.** One held-out group with one win gives p = 0.5 (exact sign test). With 5 held-out groups all won, p = 0.031 — **≥ 5 held-out groups with zero losses is the minimum** for any model to ever be promoted; the dataset has 1. `applied` events are 0 and creation pairs are absent because no walk has reached Stage 6 since the build (every walk stalled on quota).
**I10 — The "signal-derived" hotness grades still contain invented baselines.** The 33 production grades (`0.5 + (snr/40).clamp(0,0.4)`, `0.7 − …`, `0.6 + …`) carry a fixed 0.5–0.7 base that is not measurement, and their meaning (signal quality) is not hotness (use). Inert for ranking (only container-level observed hotness is read) but they make the node field mean two things. Operator ruling needed: zero the baseline / move the signal to its own field, or accept.
